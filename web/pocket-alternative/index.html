Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Zen Reader on Zen Browser and Firefox?
Yes! Zen Reader is available directly on the official Mozilla Add-ons store and installs with one click in both Firefox and Zen Browser. It also supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, and Arc.
What happens if the website goes down?
Because Zen Reader is 100% local, it has zero reliance on external backends. Even if our website or GitHub were offline, your installed extension and stored articles continue working permanently.
